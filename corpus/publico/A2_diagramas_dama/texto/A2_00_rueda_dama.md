# Rueda DAMA (The DAMA Wheel)

Transcripción literal de la Rueda DAMA de DAMA-DMBOK2. Copyright © 2024 DAMA International, CC BY-ND 4.0.

The wheel places DATA GOVERNANCE at the center, surrounded by the following knowledge areas (clockwise from the top):

- Data Modeling and Design
- Data Storage and Operations
- Data Security
- Data Integration and Interoperability
- Document and Content Management
- Reference and Master Data
- Data warehousing and business intelligence
- Metadata
- Data Quality
- Data Architecture

Center: DATA GOVERNANCE
