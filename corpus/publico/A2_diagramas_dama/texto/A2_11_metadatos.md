# Gestión de metadatos (Metadata Management)

Transcripción literal del diagrama de contexto oficial de DAMA-DMBOK2. © DAMA International, CC BY-ND 4.0.

## Definition
Planning, Implementation, and control activities that contributes to the ability to process, maintain, integrate, secure, audit and govern other data

## Goals
1. Provide organizational understanding of business terms and usage including technical lineage.
2. Collect and integrate metadata from diverse sources.
3. Provide a standard way to access metadataand enable known level of trust in data exchange.
4. Ensure metadata quality, consistency, currency, and security.
